Privacy Policy

How this website handles your information. Last updated 8 November 2026.

In short

This site does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or accounts. I do not collect personal information from visitors, except what you choose to send me yourself.

Other services this site uses

Pages load some content directly from third parties, which means your IP address and browser details reach them under their own policies:

  • GitHub: hosts this site and serves release files, project pages, images, version history and download counts.
  • Google Fonts: serves the typefaces used on this site.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare): serves the scripts that display project documents.

Stored on your device

To load faster, your browser keeps a temporary copy of some GitHub data (such as release lists and star counts) in local storage. It contains no personal information. Clearing your browser's site data removes it.

Downloads

Software is downloaded from GitHub Releases. GitHub counts downloads in total, and I show those totals. I do not track who downloads what.

When you contact me

If you email me, I receive your address and message and use them only to reply. If you use the contact form (when it is available), what you type is delivered to me on Telegram. The form does not ask for your phone number, and nothing you send is published or shared with other visitors. Telegram processes messages under its own terms.

The software itself

Each app has its own data practices, such as whether it works offline, uses an account, collects usage data or shows ads. These are listed on the app's page, along with its own privacy policy where one applies.
