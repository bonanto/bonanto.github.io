About

What this site is

Bonanto is a home for desktop software for Windows and Linux. Each app has its own page with downloads, version history, licence terms and a plain statement of how it handles your data.

The SM family

Apps that start with SM, such as SM Type Test and SM Encoder, belong to one family of tools with a shared design approach.

Licences and pricing

Apps here may be free or paid, and open or closed source. The app page always says which.
